# Questions feedback and contributions

Independent critique, corrections, and practical observations can help improve the resources. Use the Issues tab to submit feedback when it is available.

## What to include

Give the publication title and version, identify the section or PDF page, and explain the issue in plain language. If proposing a correction, include the evidence or reasoning that supports it.

For a use report, explain the setting at an appropriate level, what was tried, what was observed, and what remains uncertain. Separate measured results from expectations, estimates, and personal interpretations. Obtain any needed permission before naming an organization or person.

## Protect workplace information

Issues are public when the repository is public. Do not include confidential documents, personal employee or customer information, credentials, or details you are not authorized to disclose.

For a private permissions question, use the contact route on [myWorkforceAgents.ai](https://myworkforceagents.ai/).

## Editorial review

The author retains editorial responsibility for official publications. Suggestions may inform a future revision, but submitting a suggestion does not make it an official doctrine change.

Please use Issues for initial proposals. Any proposed edits remain subject to review before inclusion. Discussion of a translation or adaptation does not itself establish permission to distribute it; consult [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).

## Helpful issue titles

- Correction in TRAIT version 2.1 and the relevant section.
- Question about an Employee Guide scenario.
- Reported use in a particular work setting.
- Research critique with supporting sources.

Please keep discussion respectful, specific, and open to alternative explanations.
