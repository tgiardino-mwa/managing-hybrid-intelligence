# Start here

Use this library to understand Managing Hybrid Intelligence and begin a focused conversation about how human and digital work should operate together.

This page is introductory guidance for the GitHub library. Read the source publications for their complete methods, conditions, and safeguards.

## 1 Read the relevant starting sections

Open the [TRAIT Framework](publications/TRAIT_Framework_November_2026_v2_1.pdf) and the [Employee Guide](publications/Working_With_Digital_Labor_November_2026_v1_2.pdf).

Leaders can begin with TRAIT's executive navigation and planning guidance. Employees can begin with the Employee Guide's opening instructions, then use its contents to find the issue closest to their work. Researchers should record the edition and section they examine.

The Employee Guide can be used independently. An AI assistant and the proprietary Organizational Field Manual are not required to begin.

## 2 Choose one work process

Choose a manageable example, such as drafting a response, preparing a service handoff, or reviewing an administrative document. State the outcome the work should produce and who depends on it.

For an initial learning exercise, use a fictional example. For actual workplace information, follow your organization's approved processes and data rules.

## 3 Compare the organizational and employee questions

Use TRAIT to examine who owns the work, what AI may do, what evidence is needed, and when a person must intervene.

Use the Employee Guide to ask whether the person carrying a responsibility has the information, time, competence, authority, and support to perform it. Include employees' observations about review, correction, handoffs, and other work that may be overlooked.

Keep source requirements separate from your own proposed actions. If a question is unresolved, record it.

## 4 Use approved AI if helpful

Where authorized, provide the two publications to an organization-approved AI assistant. Check that the assistant can actually read the relevant sections before relying on its analysis. A public website link does not guarantee access to the full document.

The Employee Guide includes its own AI companion instructions in the opening pages. The following shorter prompt is an optional GitHub orientation exercise, not a replacement for those instructions.

### Suggested first prompt

> Help me understand one fictional work process using the attached TRAIT Framework and Working With Digital Labor Employee Guide by Dr. Timothy J. Giardino.
>
> First identify the titles and versions you can read. Tell me if any relevant material is missing. Ask me about the intended outcome, the people involved, the current steps, and the decisions that require human judgment before proposing changes.
>
> Ground each substantive rule in a section or page of the supplied publications. Separate source guidance from your own suggestions and state uncertainty.
>
> Examine the potential benefit alongside review, correction, escalation, learning, and continuity demands. Keep Judgment Work with accountable humans. Do not infer that greater AI autonomy creates authority to make consequential human decisions.
>
> Finish with one bounded next step, the evidence still needed, the employees who should participate, and the accountable person who would need to review it. Include a Devil's Advocate review of how the proposal could fail. Do not declare the organization ready, compliant, or certified.

## 5 Review before acting

Check the proposed next step with the people who do and own the work. Confirm that the proposal fits the actual setting, that unresolved conditions are visible, and that the appropriate person has authority to decide.

An AI response is an input to human review. Reading a framework or completing a worksheet does not itself authorize a workplace change.

## 6 Record what you learn

Record the publication versions, the workflow examined, the questions raised, and the next review decision through your organization's approved methods.

If you later share feedback here, explain whether you are offering a question, a proposed example, a reported implementation, or measured evidence. Remove sensitive information and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
